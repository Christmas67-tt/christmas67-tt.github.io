HistoryFactory

Short videos about battles, sieges, lost cities, ancient civilizations, natural disasters and little-known facts from history.

This app uses YouTube API Services. HistoryFactory uploads videos to its owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

What this app does

HistoryFactory is a private tool used by the owner of one YouTube channel. It prepares short vertical history videos — script, narration, illustrations and captions — and uploads them to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API.

The app is not offered to other users. It only ever signs in to the channel owner's Google account.

Images and sources

Archival images come from Wikimedia Commons, Europeana, the Library of Congress and other open collections. Only public-domain and openly licensed images that allow commercial use are used, and every video credits its sources in the description. Videos that contain AI-generated imagery are marked as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
